# Policy Document — AdTech Platform for Visitors

**Pineapple OS — Advertising & Content Integrity Rules**  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Target Audience**: Visitors only (non-school members)  

---

## 1. Zero Third-Party Ads for School Members
Active students, teachers, delegates, and administrative staff belonging to an active subscribed school see **ZERO third-party advertisements**. All ad serving requests undergo strict server-side evaluation (`has_active_school_membership`). Only visitors (unauthenticated or users without an active school membership) are eligible to receive sponsored ad cards in their visitor feed.

---

## 2. Prohibited Ad Categories
The following product and service categories are strictly prohibited from advertising on Pineapple OS:

1. **Tobacco & Nicotine**: Cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vapes, tobacco products.
2. **Alcohol & Intoxicants**: Alcoholic beverages, spirits, beer, wine.
3. **Gambling & Sports Betting**: Casinos, lotteries, online betting, poker, games of chance for money.
4. **Adult Content & Dating**: Pornography, sexually suggestive material, adult services.
5. **Weapons & Explosives**: Firearms, ammunition, knives, explosives, military gear.
6. **Unverified Health & Miracle Cures**: Unapproved pharmaceuticals, weight-loss miracle products, fraudulent medical claims.
7. **Predatory Financial Products**: Abusive loans, payday loans with exorbitant rates, multi-level marketing (MLM), pyramid schemes, high-risk crypto schemes.
8. **Unaccredited Educational Diplomas**: Fraudulent degree mills, false promises of accredited diplomas without institutional accreditation, school impersonation.
9. **Hate Speech & Discriminatory Content**: Content inciting violence, discrimination, harassment based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.
10. **Counterfeit & Illegal Goods**: Pirated software, fake luxury brands, stolen goods.

---

## 3. Labeling and Transparency Standards
- **Mandatory Label**: Every ad card served MUST display a prominent **"Sponsorisé"** badge.
- **Why This Ad Link**: Users can click "Pourquoi cette annonce ?" to see coarse targeting criteria (e.g. city/region, language, time of day).
- **Hide / Report Action**: Users can click "Masquer / Signaler cette annonce" to provide feedback or report policy violations.
- **Editorial Separation**: Advertisements must never mimic editorial school news, class announcements, or system alerts.

---

## 4. Moderation & Suspension Workflow
1. **Automated Keyword & Content Filter**: Submissions containing blacklisted keywords or suspicious destination URLs are automatically rejected at entry.
2. **Human Moderation**: All creatives require manual review (`PENDING_REVIEW` -> `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`) by a platform moderator or super-admin before entering active rotation.
3. **Automatic Threshold Suspension**: Any campaign receiving more than 5 user flags/reports is automatically paused and moved to the moderation queue for re-evaluation.
4. **Advertiser Suspension**: Repeated policy violations lead to advertiser account suspension and forfeiture of unspent campaign allocations.
